Frequently asked questions

Does the bot issue official grades?

No. It provides AI-generated formative feedback based on assessment rubrics, not official assessment decisions.

What should I provide?

Provide the relevant assessment rubric and work you are authorized to share. Avoid unnecessary personal or confidential information.

What if I do not have a rubric?

The bot should request a rubric rather than invent assessment criteria.

What if the feedback is inaccurate?

Compare it with the rubric and consult your educator for authoritative guidance.

Are my submissions automatically stored in Google Drive?

No. The inspected version does not have an operational Google Drive upload integration. Verified email uploads and Google Drive storage are planned features, not currently available.

Can the publisher see my ChatGPT submission?

The publisher reports no independent receipt or access to submissions through the current bot. ChatGPT processing and account or workspace access are governed by the applicable platform arrangements. See the Privacy Policy.

How can I request deletion?

Email limidris@gmail.com regarding information controlled by the publisher. For content held by ChatGPT, use the relevant platform account and privacy controls. A request to the publisher does not itself delete platform-held content.

Availability

The service is intended for adults aged 18 or above. Availability may depend on ChatGPT access and platform requirements.
