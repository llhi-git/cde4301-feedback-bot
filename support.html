Frequently asked questions

Can the bot assign an official grade?

No. It provides formative feedback only.

What if I have no rubric?

Provide the relevant assessment rubric before asking for an evaluation.

What if feedback seems wrong?

Compare it with the rubric and ask an educator to clarify.
