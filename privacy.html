1. Scope and intended users

CDE4301 Interim Feedback Bot is an AI-assisted tool intended to provide formative feedback on submitted work against assessment rubrics. It is intended for adults aged 18 and above, including university students and educators. It does not provide official grades or replace an educator's judgment.

2. Information users provide

To receive feedback, users may provide assessment work, rubrics, questions and related context. Those materials may contain personal information if users include it. Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive or confidential information and should only submit materials they are authorized to use.

3. How the current bot uses information

The current bot uses user-provided materials to generate rubric-aligned feedback and suggestions for improvement. The publisher has not identified a separate collection, analytics, research or secondary-use system in the inspected plugin package. This statement does not describe or override ChatGPT's own processing practices.

4. Current storage and access

The inspected plugin package contains rubric-feedback instructions and reference material, but no Google Drive upload integration or independent submission-storage workflow. The publisher reports that they do not independently receive or access users' submissions through the current bot. This does not imply that conversations or uploaded files are inaccessible to the ChatGPT service or to anyone who may have access under the user's account or workspace arrangements.

ChatGPT may process and retain conversations and uploaded files under the terms, privacy notices, settings and controls applicable to the user's account or workspace. Users should consult the applicable ChatGPT notices and controls for details.

5. Planned Google Drive integration

An optional feature for verified public uploads to a publisher-controlled Google Drive account is under development. It is not currently implemented or available in the inspected bot. No statement in this policy should be read as confirmation that submissions are automatically transferred to Google Drive. Before any such feature launches, this policy must be revised to describe the actual authentication, consent, recipients, storage, retention, security and deletion arrangements.

6. Retention and deletion

Because the publisher reports no independent receipt or storage of student submissions through the current bot, no separate publisher-managed retention period for those submissions has been established. ChatGPT-held content is governed by the applicable platform terms and settings, not by a deletion request to the publisher alone.

For questions or requests about information actually controlled by the publisher, email limidris@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed manually, with authorization checks where appropriate. The publisher cannot promise deletion of data held by ChatGPT or another independent service. No fixed response or deletion timeframe is promised in this draft.

7. Security, sharing and international processing

The inspected plugin does not expose a separate Google Drive storage integration. Processing through ChatGPT may involve the platform's service providers and infrastructure under the applicable terms and privacy notices. The publisher has not verified additional independent storage, sharing or cross-border processing by the current plugin. Any new backend or storage provider will require an updated disclosure before launch.

8. Privacy enquiries and rights

Users may contact the publisher regarding information the publisher actually controls and to raise privacy questions. Rights to access, correct, or delete information depend on applicable law and who controls the relevant data. For ChatGPT account data, users should use the applicable platform privacy and account controls.

9. Updates

The publisher should update this policy if the bot's data-handling practices change, especially before enabling external storage or verified uploads. The proposed effective date is 1 November 2026, subject to publisher approval.
