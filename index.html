CDE4301 Interim Feedback Bot helps students review interim assessment work against supplied assessment rubrics and receive specific, actionable formative feedback.

What it does

  • Identifies rubric criteria met, partially met, or not met.
  • Explains judgments using evidence from submitted work.
  • Suggests improvements toward higher rubric performance levels.
  • Requests the rubric if one is unavailable or unreadable.

How to use

Open the bot in ChatGPT, supply the relevant rubric and your work, then request an evaluation or improvement suggestions.

Important

Feedback is AI-generated, may be inaccurate, and is not an official grade. Do not submit confidential or unnecessary personal information. Automatic Google Drive storage is not a verified feature of the current plugin.
