1. Purpose and intended users

CDE4301 Interim Feedback Bot is intended for adults aged 18 or above who want formative, rubric-based feedback on assessment work. It does not provide official grades or replace an educator's academic judgment.

2. Responsible use

Users should submit only materials they are authorized to use, follow applicable academic-integrity and institutional requirements, and avoid unnecessary sensitive or confidential personal information.

3. Accuracy and reliance

AI-generated feedback may contain errors, omissions, or misinterpretations. Users should verify suggestions against the original rubric and consult an educator for official assessment decisions.

4. Current functionality

The current bot provides rubric-based feedback through ChatGPT. It does not currently offer automatic Google Drive submission storage or verified-email upload accounts. These features are under development and will require updated disclosures and terms before launch.

5. Charges and access

The publisher does not intend to charge a separate fee for use of the bot. ChatGPT access, subscriptions, usage limits, or other platform conditions may still apply.
